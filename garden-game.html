What the game is teaching

🌱 Right plant

Thirsty plants (tomato, mint, fern) enjoy daily water. Succulents and cacti need dry spells.

🪴 Right soil

Compost and loam hold water; sand and perlite drain it. Match the mix to the plant's roots.

💧 Right rhythm

Too much love drowns roots as surely as too little dries them. Watch the moisture bar.
