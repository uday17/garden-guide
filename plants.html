How to choose a plant

Match the light first

A plant in the wrong light will never thrive, no matter how well you water it. Use the “Light” line on each card and compare it with your space.

Be honest about time

Succulents and snake plants forgive a fortnight of neglect. Ferns and roses do not. The level dots show how demanding each plant really is.

Indoor growing

Making a room plant-friendly

Group plants to raise humidity, keep them out of draughts and radiators, and rotate pots a quarter turn weekly so they grow evenly. In winter, move them closer to the window and water far less.

Outdoor growing

Balconies, beds and walls

Outdoor plants face wind, rain and temperature swings. Use larger pots for stability, mulch the surface, and choose climbers for vertical space — a railing can hold three times the plants of a floor.
